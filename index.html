Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to use?

Yes. Foreground only reads information already on your Mac — which programs start on their own — and never modifies, disables, or deletes anything unless you explicitly choose to elsewhere.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. App recognition uses a database built into the app itself: it works offline, on a plane, or without Wi-Fi.

Does it need administrator access?

No. Foreground only reads what your own user account can already see — it will never ask for your admin password to run a scan.

Which Macs does it work on?

macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
